# From-The-Depths-Manual-Archipelago
Manual Archipelago ap world and yaml for From The Depths. I have never done github syntax, so I apologize for this being just rows of text without sorting! Also, while I can verify that this AP world works (generates correctly, manual client works with it, etc.), there has been no actual playtests yet and there are no current options for it. This is my first manual AP world.

This is a Manual Archipelago for the Adventure Mode in From The Depths (FTD). Adventure mode starts you on a small wooden raft and has you slowly building up materials to build a progressively bigger and more powerful craft, effectively as an endless mode. There is a "soft victory" in reaching the max difficulty (100) and / or defeating one of the largest crafts in the game, but there is no true "victory screen".

This manual archipelago randomizes when you unlock various building tabs, the sizes of weapons you can build, how much your craft can cost, when you unlock various munitions and weapon parts, and when you can build subvehicles, as well as some other stuff.

There are 10 "Progressive Cost" items (progression) and three "Extra Cost" items (useful). The amount of materials your vehicle can cost is capped at the following for each Progressive Cost item. The numbers are loosely based on the most expensive enemy craft in the regions you would unlock by having a Progressive Cost item.

0 Progressive Costs (Starting out): 50K

1 Progressive Cost: 150K

2 Progressive Costs: 350K

3 Progressive Costs: 500K

4 Progressive Costs: 750K

5 Progressive Costs: 1 Million

6 Progressive Costs: 1.25 Million

7 Progressive Costs: 1.5 Million

8 Progressive Costs: 1.75 Million

9 Progressive Costs: 2 Million

10 Progressive Costs: 2.5 Million

Each "Extra Cost" will add +5% to your current progressive cost. So, if your max cost is currently 150K, and you have one Extra Cost, you now have a max cost of 157.5K.
Each Subvehicle may cost 5% of your max cost at that time, and each Progressive Subvehicle Cost increases that by 5%, to a max of 25%.

The rest of the items should be fairly self-explanatory.
